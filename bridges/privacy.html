Bridges: privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026 · Back to the game

Who is responsible

Bridges is a research game by Sofia Gallego, run by CosmoVentures SASU, France, which is responsible for the data (the data controller). Contact: sofiag.gallego@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your answers: the bridges you write, the stepping stones you add, and the words in the warm-up.
  • How you play: how long answers take, whether text was typed, spoken or pasted, which buttons you use (for example whether you asked for or saw an AI's answer, and whether you changed your own), and the difficulty you chose.
  • Optional questions at the end: whether you played before, use of AI tools, how you feel about AI, your first language, an age range and a broad field.
  • A random player code (such as river-bee-42) that groups your answers. It is kept on your device so you can come back.

We do not collect your name, IP address or location, and the game uses no cookies and no third-party trackers. We only count how many times the page is opened from each country, as daily totals that are never linked to your answers.

Voice

If you choose to speak, your recording is sent once to a speech-to-text model running in our own Cloudflare account, turned into text, and discarded straight away. Only the text is kept.

Feedback and results email

Feedback (stars and comments) is stored without your player code and with the date only, so it cannot be linked to your answers. If you leave an email for the results, it goes to a separate list with no code and no date, is used once to send the results, and is then deleted.

Why, and on what basis

The answers are used for a research study on how people connect distant ideas, compared with AI systems. We process them on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can stop at any time.

Where the data is kept, and for how long

The data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database (primary location in western Europe). Cloudflare acts as our processor and may handle data outside the EU under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Answers are kept with their player codes until 31 December 2027. After that the codes are removed. An anonymous dataset, with no codes and nothing that identifies anyone, may be published for other researchers.

Your rights

With your player code you can see your bridges and delete all your answers at any time from the game (“See my bridges”, then “Delete all my answers”). Without the code we cannot tell which answers are yours, because we never know who you are. You can also write to sofiag.gallego@gmail.com about access, correction or deletion, and you have the right to complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Bridges is for people aged 18 and over.
